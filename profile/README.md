# RdeV - River Valley High School Development Club

Welcome to **RdeV**, a branch of the Infocomm club of River Valley High School. We are a group of builders who look for problems (or create them) and make solutions for them. Our expertise range from game dev, web dev, app dev and even creating hardware solutions.
## Our Projects

### 🤝 SCS Webiste
SCS contacted us to make a website. And so we did!

### 🌿 EcoTrail Website
We created the website for the RV **EcoTrail**, designed to promote environmental awareness within the school community.

## Join Us
We are always looking for passionate developers and creative minds to join our club. If you're from River Valley High School and interested in software development and want to make a difference, feel free to reach out!
